Signs You Need Termite Control in Totowa Section

Recognizing the signs of termite activity is crucial for timely intervention. In Totowa Section, homeowners should be vigilant for specific indicators that signal the need for Termite Control services.

  • Mud tubes on walls

    Termites build mud tubes to travel and protect themselves from predators. Finding these tubes is a clear sign of infestation.

  • Hollow-sounding wood

    If you tap on wood and it sounds hollow, it may indicate termites have eaten away at the interior, compromising structural integrity.

  • Frass (termite droppings)

    Finding frass near wooden structures indicates active termite feeding. This is a sign that immediate treatment is necessary.

  • Swarming insects

    Seeing swarming termites, especially in spring, is a strong indicator of an established colony nearby.

  • Damaged wood structures

    Visible damage to wooden structures, such as sagging or crumbling wood, suggests a serious termite problem that needs urgent attention.
