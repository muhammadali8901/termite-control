Signs You Need Termite Control in Hillcrest

Recognizing the signs of a termite infestation is crucial for homeowners in Hillcrest. If you notice any of the following symptoms, it may be time to call for termite control services.

  • Mud tubes on walls

    Termites create mud tubes to travel between their nests and food sources. Finding these tubes is a clear indication of an infestation.

  • Wood damage or hollow-sounding wood

    If you notice wood that appears damaged or sounds hollow when tapped, it may indicate termite activity.

  • Swarmers or discarded wings

    Seeing swarmers or finding discarded wings inside your home can signal a termite colony nearby.

  • Bubbling or peeling paint

    Bubbling or peeling paint can be a sign of moisture damage caused by termites tunneling beneath the surface.

  • Visible termite droppings

    Termite droppings, often resembling sawdust, can indicate an active infestation and should be addressed immediately.

How Seasons Affect Termite Control in Hillcrest

The changing seasons in Hillcrest significantly influence termite activity and the need for control measures. Understanding these seasonal patterns helps us provide timely services.

Spring

As the weather warms, termite activity increases as they emerge to forage for food.

Tip: Schedule a thorough inspection in early spring to catch any potential infestations before they escalate.

Summer

Summer heat can exacerbate termite problems, as they thrive in warm conditions.

Tip: Ensure your property is treated regularly to combat the increased risk of infestations.

Fall

Termites may swarm in the fall as they search for new nesting sites.

Tip: Keep an eye out for swarmers and schedule inspections to address any new threats.

Winter

While termite activity slows in winter, they do not hibernate and can still cause damage.

Tip: Consider winter inspections to catch any hidden infestations before the spring thaw.
