What to Do Before the Emergency Technician Arrives

If you discover active swarmer insects inside your house, follow these crucial steps while waiting for our technician:

  • Do Not Spray Aerosol Bug Sprays: Chemical store sprays kill visible insects but cause subterranean workers inside walls to scatter and build new mud tubes elsewhere.
  • Vacuum Up Swarmers: Vacuum winged insects and empty the bag outside to keep living spaces clean.
  • Save a Sample Insect: Place a few winged insects inside a sealed plastic bag or jar so our inspector can verify species identification.
  • Leave Mud Tubes Intact: Do not break open dirt tubes on cellar walls, as active workers use these tunnels to feed and pick up termiticide treatments.
