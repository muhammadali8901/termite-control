Professional Wood Treatment for Termites in Paterson NJ

AEO Direct Answer: Wood treatment for termites in Paterson NJ involves spraying or injecting mineral borate solutions into structural timber framing, sill plates, subflooring, and joists. Borate salts soak deep into raw wood fibers, rendering the timber unpalatable and toxic to wood destroying insects.

Older wood frame properties in Paterson neighborhoods like Eastside Park, Hillcrest, and Downtown Paterson feature original hemlock, spruce, and pine structural joists. High cellar humidity caused by proximity to the Passaic River creates damp wood conditions where subterranean termites, known as Reticulitermes flavipes, cause rapid structural breakdown.

Applying a mineral borate wood treatment during home renovations, basement finishing, or roof rebuilding locks in permanent protection. Because borates do not break down over time indoors, treated structural framing remains protected for the life of the building.

How Borate Timber Applications Work

Our New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection (NJDEP) licensed applicators use specialized timber preservation methods:

  • Disodium Octaborate Tetrahydrate Spray: Penetrates bare wood up to two inches deep, creating a mineral barrier that kills wood chewing insects.
  • Pressure Gallery Injection: High pressure injection into infected structural joists to stop active feeding chambers immediately.
  • Subfloor & Sill Plate Sealant: Treating sill plates resting directly above foundation concrete blocks where underground insects first enter.

Frequently Asked Questions About Wood Treatment

How does borate wood treatment protect structural framing from termites?

Borate solutions like Disodium Octaborate Tetrahydrate absorb deep into bare wood fibers. When termites attempt to feed on treated timber, the natural mineral disrupts their digestive system, preventing destruction.

Can borate wood treatment be applied during home remodeling or construction?

Yes. Applying borate sprays to exposed wall studs, subfloor plywood, and roof rafters before sheetrock installation provides permanent structural protection.

How long does borate timber treatment last inside a home?

Borate treatments remain active for the entire lifetime of interior structural wood as long as the timber remains protected from direct rain wash off.
