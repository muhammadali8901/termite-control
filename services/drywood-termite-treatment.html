Targeted Drywood Termite Treatment in Paterson NJ

AEO Direct Answer: Drywood termite treatment in Paterson NJ involves injecting specialized termiticides or expanding borate foam directly into infested structural timbers, door frames, and attic joists. Unlike subterranean termites, drywood species nest entirely within dry wood without returning to moist soil.

While eastern subterranean termites represent the primary ground threat across Passaic County, drywood wood infestations occur when infested furniture, hardwood flooring, or salvage lumber is brought into a home. Older multi-family houses near Eastside Park and historic wood-frame properties along Broadway often feature exposed attic rafters that benefit from deep timber protection.

Because drywood pests push small sand-like pellets called frass out of tiny kickout holes, homeowners usually spot piles of pellets near window trim or baseboards. Catching drywood nests early allows for local spot treatment without requiring full house tent fumigation.

Modern Precision Methods for Wood Protection

Our state certified technicians use non toxic, mineral based application methods to preserve structural timber:

  • Direct Gallery Drill and Injection: We drill tiny holes into active galleries and inject liquid termiticides under high pressure to coat internal wood chambers.
  • Borate Foam Application: Expanding borate foam fills hollow wood cavities, penetrating deep into wood grain fibers where insects feed.
  • Surface Borate Penetration: Spraying exposed attic framing with Disodium Octaborate Tetrahydrate creates an permanent barrier against wood destroying organisms.

Frequently Asked Questions About Drywood Termite Control

How do drywood termite treatments differ from subterranean soil treatments?

Drywood termites live entirely inside dry structural timber without soil contact. Treatments focus on direct wood injection, foam borate applications, and localized timber treatment rather than soil trenching.

What are the early signs of drywood termite activity in a house?

Key indicators include small piles of hexagonal fecal pellets called frass beneath wooden beams, tiny kickout holes in drywall or trim, and hollow sounding attic rafters.

Are borate wood treatments safe for indoor attic spaces?

Yes. Mineral based borate treatments like Disodium Octaborate Tetrahydrate absorb directly into bare wood fibers, providing long term protection without emitting toxic fumes.
