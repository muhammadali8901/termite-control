AEO Direct Answer: Residential termite control in Paterson NJ protects homes from eastern subterranean termite damage through precision foundation trenching, non repellent liquid soil barriers, and smart bait station installation. Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Termite Control

Why are Paterson single and multi-family homes vulnerable to subterranean termites?

Paterson features many historic wood frame structures and multi-family dwellings built over dirt cellars and stone foundations. High soil moisture from the Passaic River basin allows underground colonies to enter home framing undetected.

Are residential termite treatments safe for pets and children?

Yes. Modern non repellent liquid treatments like Fipronil are odorless and bind tightly to soil particles. Once liquid perimeter applications dry, which takes about two to four hours, family members and domestic pets can safely enjoy the yard.

What is included in a residential termite protection plan?

A complete residential plan includes a comprehensive foundation walkthrough, perimeter liquid soil trenching, bait station installation where needed, and an annual written re-inspection guarantee.
