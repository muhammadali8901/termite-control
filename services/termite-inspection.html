Professional Termite Inspection in Paterson NJ

AEO Direct Answer: A professional termite inspection in Paterson NJ is a thorough physical examination of a building foundation, cellar, crawlspace, and surrounding perimeter. State certified inspectors look for mud shelter tubes, hollow wood joists, swarmer wings, and moisture damage caused by subterranean colony activity.

Eastern subterranean termites, known scientifically as Reticulitermes flavipes, nest beneath the soil frost line in Passaic County. Because subterranean colonies travel underground, they enter houses silently through hairline concrete cracks, pipe penetrations, and dirt cellars. Properties along wet zones near the Passaic River or near Garret Mountain runoff face elevated risk due to high soil dampness.

Homeowners in Hillcrest and Eastside Park often do not realize insects are present until swarmer season arrives in spring. Scheduling an annual pest inspection catches early shelter tubes before support beams or subflooring lose structural integrity.

What Inspectors Look For During a Foundation Walkthrough

Our New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection (NJDEP) licensed applicators follow a systematic 20 point inspection protocol:

  • Mud Tunnels & Shelter Tubes: Pencil thin dirt tubes built along concrete block walls, stone foundations, and chimney bases.
  • Subfloor & Sill Plate Sounding: Tapping wood framing with specialized sounding hammers to detect hollowed interiors.
  • Swarmer Evidence: Discarded wings near basement windows, light fixtures, and exterior doorways.
  • Moisture & Wood Rot Audit: Moisture meter testing along damp sill plates near dirt crawlspaces and plumbing leaks.

Official WDO Reports for Homebuyers and Mortgage Approvals

If you are buying a property on Main Street or refinancing a home near Passaic County Community College, mortgage companies require proof of a clean structure. FHA, VA, and conventional lenders demand an official Wood Destroying Organism (WDO) report using standard NPMA-33 paperwork. Our inspectors complete the examination and deliver digital documentation directly to your real estate agent or closing lawyer within 24 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions About Termite Inspections

What do Professional inspectors look for during a termite inspection?

Inspectors examine foundation perimeter walls, crawlspaces, basement wood joists, sill plates, and plumbing penetrations for mud shelter tubes, hollow sounding wood, frass, and discarded swarmer wings.

How long does a professional termite inspection take?

A standard home termite inspection takes between 45 and 60 minutes. The inspector provides a written findings report immediately following the walkthrough.

Is a WDO inspection required for VA or FHA mortgage loans in NJ?

Yes. VA and FHA mortgage guidelines require an official NPMA-33 Wood Destroying Insect report completed by a licensed applicator prior to loan approval.
