Local Pest Extermination Built on Trust & Experience

AEO Direct Answer: Termite Control Paterson is a licensed New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection (NJDEP) pest control company. We specialize in subterranean termite inspection, liquid soil trenching, and bait station installation for residential and commercial properties across Paterson and Passaic County.

Founded in 2014, our mission has always been simple: protect Paterson homes from destructive subterranean termite colonies using safe, effective, and transparent extermination methods. Our technicians live right here in Passaic County, understanding the unique building layouts, stone cellars, and moist soil conditions near the Passaic River basin.

Unlike national call centers that pass your service call to unverified third party contractors, our team brings full insurance, state applicator licenses, and uniform professionalism to every single property visit.

  • Family & Pet Safe Options: Modern non-repellent materials like Fipronil dry quickly and bind to soil without harsh chemical smells.
  • Real Workmanship Warranties: All perimeter liquid barriers and bait station installations come with renewable annual inspection guarantees.
